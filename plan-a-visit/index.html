Trying a new church can be stressful. We want to help.

Here's everything you need to know before your first Sunday, from where to park to what your kids will do.

Here's how Sunday mornings work.

What to expect

Family-friendly, Bible-centered, and relaxed.

  • Preaching is Bible-centered and practical. Bring a Bible, or grab a free one at the Welcome Center.
  • Worship is blended: contemporary songs and fresh arrangements of hymns, led by a band and singers.
  • No offering plates. Offering boxes are in the lobby and Welcome Center. Guests are never expected to give.
  • Dress is mostly casual or business casual. Your being here matters more than what you wear.
Parking

Reserved for guests

First-time guest spaces are right by the main entrance, with handicap parking nearby.

Kids

Safe and fun

Nursery during all services, Sunday School at 9:30, and Children's Church at 10:30. Every worker is a screened and trained adult.

Arriving

Come a few minutes early

Give yourself time to park and find your way. Greeters will help you find a Sunday School class for your family.

Wednesdays

Kids & teens

Temple Kids and the Teen Ministry meet Wednesday nights in the Family Life Center.

Finding us

East side of Connersville, off State Road 44.

1380 IN-44, Connersville, IN 47331

  • Main building: Worship Center, café, classrooms, and church offices.
  • Family Life Center: Temple Kids, children's Sunday School rooms, Team K.I.D., the gym, kitchen, and Teen Ministry.
