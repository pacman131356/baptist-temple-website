About us

We're committed to serving and nurturing every member, so our ministry supports the spiritual journey of every age.

Our mission

Three things we're about.

What we believe

Our statement of faith.

Questions about anything here? We'd love to talk with you.

Contact UsPlan a Visit
God

There is one God, eternally existing in three equal persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. He is the Creator, unchanging, holy, loving, merciful, and just, and He will judge the world.

The Bible

The Old and New Testaments were written by men inspired by the Holy Spirit. The Bible is God's revelation of Himself, completely true and trustworthy, and all of it points to Jesus Christ.

Man

People were created in God's image and without sin. Through Adam's fall, sin and spiritual death passed to all humanity, so no one can come to God apart from His grace.

Jesus

Jesus is the eternal Son of God, fully God and fully man, born of the Virgin Mary. He died on the cross in our place, rose bodily from the dead, ascended to heaven, and will return to judge and reign.

The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit is fully God. He inspired Scripture, exalts Christ, and convicts the world. He brings new birth, places believers into the body of Christ, seals, sanctifies, and comforts them, and gives spiritual gifts to the church.

Salvation

Salvation redeems the whole person and is offered freely to all who repent and trust in Jesus. It is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone.

Assurance of Believers

Those who are truly redeemed are kept secure in Christ forever by God's power. Salvation can't be earned or lost by what we do, and that assurance leads to joyful obedience.

The Church

The church is the body and bride of Christ, made up of all believers with Jesus as its head. Local churches are independent and led by pastors under Christ's authority, and observe two ordinances: believer's baptism and the Lord's Supper. Our mission is to share the gospel with every nation, starting with our neighbors.
