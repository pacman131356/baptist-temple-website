Temple Kids · Birth to 6th grade

The best part of their week.

A place where your child can laugh and play, learn and grow, make friends, and most importantly, discover God's great love for them.

Why parents love it

Fun, safe, and relational.

Our goal is to make Sundays and Wednesdays the best days of your child's week, so they can meet Jesus on their level.

01 · Safe

Secure check-in

Every child is checked in with a matching pick-up receipt. Volunteers are highly trained and screened with several background checks, and our safety team routinely patrols the building.

02 · Fun

Joy you can hear

We find our joy in Jesus, so we put a big emphasis on fun. Songs, games, crafts, and lessons all teach kids about God and His love for them.

03 · Gospel-focused

Jesus at the center

We gather to worship the God who wants a relationship with us, and we make sure your child understands that every Sunday they're with us.

Our kids programs

Something every week of the year.

Sundays · 9:30 AM

Sunday School

Age-based classes from the nursery through 6th grade, in kid-friendly rooms in the main building and the Family Life Center.

Sundays · 10:30 AM

Kids Church

For elementary-age kids. We meet in the Temple Kids Room during the morning service for our own worship, Bible study, games, and more.

Wednesdays · 6:30 PM

Temple Kids

During the school year, 6:30 to 8:00 PM: a free dinner every week, plus lessons, games, crafts, Bible memory, and more.

Summers · 6:30 PM

Summer Programs

When Temple Kids is on break for the summer, we keep the fun going with other kids activities from 6:30 to 8:00 PM.

Summers · Usually July

Vacation Bible School

A fun-packed week of music, games, Bible study, crafts, skits, and so much more. Get ready for a wild week every summer.

First time?

We'll help you get checked in.

Arrive a few minutes early and a greeter will walk you to the right room. The nursery is in the main building, off the lobby.
