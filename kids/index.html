Temple Kids

The best part of their week.

From birth through 6th grade, we want Sundays and Wednesdays to be the best days of your child's week: fun, safe, and full of the gospel.

Safe

Secure check-in

Every child is checked in with a matching pick-up receipt. Volunteers are trained and pass several background checks, and safety staff routinely patrol the building.

Fun

Songs, games & crafts

Kids learn through songs, games, crafts, and lessons built for their age.

Gospel-centered

Jesus at the center

Every lesson points kids to God's love for them and their relationship with Him.
