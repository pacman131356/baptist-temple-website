Adults & outreach

Find your people. Serve your neighbors.

There's a place for you to grow and serve at Baptist Temple. For details, call the church office or check the weekly bulletin.

For adults

Grow together.

Groups and classes for every season of life.

  1. 01

    Worship

    Sundays, 10:30 AM

    Biblically centered, Christ-honoring music in a blended style of contemporary and traditional songs.

  2. 02

    Joshua's Men

    Saturdays, 7 AM

    A men's discipleship group with a verse-by-verse Bible study, building godly men who lead their families and church.

  3. 03

    Women's Ministry

    Connecting women to Jesus and to one another through conferences, crafting, Bible studies, and fellowship and service events. All women are welcome.

  4. 04

    Senior Adults

    Events and activities for senior adults. Check the bulletin or call the church office for what's coming up.

  5. 05

    Sonshiner's

    Sundays, 10:30 AM · Room 113

    A class for adults with special needs: Bible verses, trivia with prizes, prayer, singing, and refreshments. Anyone is welcome to join.

Outreach

Reach out.

Ways we care for Connersville and the world.

  1. 06

    Community Outreach

    A food pantry (bring donations to the church office), Friends of the Lord's Table serving meals two Monday nights a month, and Mats for the Homeless on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 1 PM, January through April.

  2. 07

    International Missions

    Spreading the gospel locally and around the world and supporting missionary families. See the weekly Missionary Spotlight in the bulletin.

  3. 08

    Video & Live Streaming

    Every Sunday

    Our T3 team streams worship each week so people who can't be there in person can still worship and study with us.

  4. 09

    Bus & Van Ministry

    Call to schedule

    Need a ride to church? Call the office and we'll arrange a pickup.
